The vision

We are transforming how we social network.

We know we can only be truly successful together. So we've built the technology to support us all in our local and mutual thriving.

Rivr is more than just an app. It is a technology to enhance connection and stewardship in our local bio-regions, worldwide. Whether you're offering your hands or providing a service, hosting a gathering or joining an event, stewarding a community or simply seeking connection, the Rivr app makes it seamless to give and receive what we want and need — all within a living web of mutual support, all for the prayer of our collective wellness, and for the stewardship of this Earth.

At the heart of Rivr lies a profound belief: that the essence of our existence lives not in competition, but in collaboration. We know, to truly be well as a collective — as a culture, as a bio-region, as a world — we must put the earth, the people, and our hearts in the center. It's time for us to coordinate in ways we've been yearning for; to be well together in ways we've never seen before; to live life beautifully; to weave and honor the rich soil of land and love, for all those here and for the next generations to come.

And so Rivr is federated. Every neighborhood, group, and bioregion runs its own sovereign home — its own domain, its own data, its own rules — and they all flow together into one river, so neighbors can find each other across communities. You can leave any time and take everything with you. That's the point.

Together, we are destined for greatness.

Our vision is a collective vision.
