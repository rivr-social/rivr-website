Our journey

A vision born from immediate community needs.

Rivr emerged from ONE Boulder (2020–2023), a prior community network that has since dissolved. The Rivr name was adopted in June 2026, and Rivr is a new entity. From the start, the vision has been to create a place for people to mutually support one another, share offerings and events, coordinate gifts and resources, and weave a beautiful heart-connected collective.

We envision a world where people are not isolated, but feel their inherent belonging; where souls find shelter, safety and connection; and where purpose can be embodied in relationship with people and place.

We are here on purpose, for mutual thriving, so we can collaborate more efficiently as bioregions around the world. Everyone has a part to play.

The best way to know the Rivr is to get in it.

We welcome your feedback and inspirations along the way.
