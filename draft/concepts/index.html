Critical read of the current draft

From telling the story
to letting people enter it.

The current draft has a strong visual voice, a memorable premise, and a useful life → community → world progression. Its central weakness is behavioral: it still presents one continuous story to every visitor. The relevant audience signal arrives late, the altitude cards do not yet carry a chosen identity forward, and depth is determined by page order rather than visitor intent.

01 / Tidal

Highest emotional pull

Best continuation of the existing Rivr aesthetic. Immersive and memorable, though it can feel abstract if the practical copy is not kept close.

02 / Field

Highest public clarity

The easiest direction for a broad audience to trust and understand. Human and editorial, though less visibly “networked” at first glance.

03 / Atlas

Strongest systems story

Most clearly expresses federation, place, and infrastructure. Distinctive and rigorous, though it risks feeling technical to casual visitors.

Three expressions. One invitation: start from where you are.
