A new kind of tech (r)evolution

Community exchange made seamless.

Rivr helps you create and manage organizations, projects, roles and tasks to find or offer the perfect skill for any need. By connecting locally, you build resilience, support projects and weave a stronger community fabric.

Every local exchange compounds your community’s wealth and puts a greater stake in your homeland. We believe all people deserve the means to create a beautiful life and a real connection to their community.

The app is still in active development. New and unique social coordination features will roll out over time, alongside an ever smoother and more refined experience.
