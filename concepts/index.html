Corrected information architecture

Products on the surface.
Altitudes in the journey.

The earlier concepts made the altitude itself the choice. That was backwards: people do not arrive thinking “daily life, community, or world.” They recognize themselves as a participant, host, seller, provider, or organization. The product is the doorway; the three altitudes explain why that product matters at progressively larger scales.

01 / Catalog

Best for confident comparison

All five products are immediately visible with pricing and a capability matrix. Strongest when visitors already understand what kind of work they need to do.

02 / Finder

Best for segment recognition

Visitors choose the sentence that sounds like their role and receive a focused recommendation. Strongest public front door for people new to Rivr.

03 / Journeys

Best for the complete thesis

A selected product visibly crosses life, community, and world. Strongest expression of how practical software becomes social infrastructure.

04 / Comparison

Best for purchase confidence

Each subscription is measured against the specialist stack it consolidates, with current prices, fee scenarios, methodology, and primary sources.

Five products. Three altitudes. One connected social fabric.
