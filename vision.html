Economic revolution

Put power back in the hands of the people. Literally.

If you can find what you need inside your community, if you can provide what your community needs from you, and if we can too, then together we can increase resilience and human connection. With every nearby transaction, you compound your community’s wealth and put more stake into your homeland.

Rivr’s coordination services help organizations, events, groups and individuals find the right people to execute projects. Create groups, projects, roles and tasks; then match skills with meaningful reciprocation.

This means more sovereignty in how we use time, resources and attention; more authentic connection across people, land and culture; and more creative liberty in how we build beautiful lives.

Our vision

Every interaction blooms with potential.

“Another world is not only possible, she is on her way. On a quiet day, I can hear her breathing.” — Arundhati Roy

We envision a society where the circulation of skills, services and gratitude forms the cornerstone of interaction. With today’s technology, this becomes possible through a platform that celebrates diversity, fosters inclusion, encourages generosity, and honors maturation and the human journey.

This is more than an app. It is a movement toward a world where every exchange strengthens the webs that bind us and every connection enriches our capacity to give and receive love.

We know the only way we will make a more beautiful world for all is together.

We are a tribal, social species. Humanity came this far because we knew the importance of sharing resources and stewarding relationship with the land. We do not need to throw technology away to return to a more original way of being; we can use it, along with the lessons of our collective maturation, to make collaboration more effective than ever.

The foundation of our mission is to connect people with the things humans really need: the true nourishing things. Community is built in day-to-day relationships—not only by dancing together, but by doing the gritty parts of life together too.

We want a shared context where the world makes sense, where we hold roles we are proud of, share the way, and feel welcomed and at home.
