Spreading our wings

Boulder is the first branch of many.

As the world evolves, Rivr evolves too—becoming a vibrant social coordination platform that empowers deeper engagement in the communities we cherish, globally.

As we share this technology with new places, we are enhancing our collective ability to manage projects, events and organizations while broadening our cultural horizon. We are here for integration, collective maturation and the diversity of experience necessary for a whole ecosystem of humans to thrive.

Places we are preparing for include Austin, Portland, Eugene, Ashland, Boston and Western Massachusetts, Asheville, Nashville, the San Francisco Bay Area, San Diego, Ojai, Los Angeles, Tahoe, Sedona, Taos, Nosara, Santa Teresa, Uvita and more.

Expanding on the greater vision

Here on purpose, for mutual thriving.

The birthing of Rivr, though new unto itself, emerged from the immediate needs of community in 2020. After years of success and years of learning, the original ONE|Boulder app was laid to rest and Rivr was born to take its place. We honor that journey because the prayer for collective evolution brought us together from the start.

This vision is of a more beautiful, interconnected and thriving world: one where people feel their inherent belonging; where souls find shelter, safety and connection; and where purpose can be embodied in relationship with people and place.

Rivr creates a digital place for community to mutually support one another, share offerings and events, coordinate gifts and resources, and weave a heart-connected collective.
